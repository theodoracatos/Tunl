Share the run, not the score

End a great run and send a card of the tunnel you actually flew - the corridor narrowing, the exact spot you died, and your personal best marked just ahead of it. Everyone gets the same cave that day, so anyone you send it to can go straight at your number.

Race your own ghost

Every ship, a real build choice

8 ships, each pairing a buff with a drawback, from the free PEARL starter up through AMBER, CRIMSON, ELECTRIC, TOXIC, VOID, NOVA, and the rare SOLARIS (wide near-miss window, larger hitbox). Fly one enough and it gains mastery, easing its drawback and growing its buff.

Stalactites, mines, boulders, cannons

Sharp stalactites, paired chicanes, spikes that shake loose and fall, boulders that make you commit up or down, and rare wall-mounted cannons that open fire as you close in. Each new stretch of cave brings a new threat, and the run keeps speeding up.

Eight Ships, Eight Feels

Same one-button feel, different tradeoffs.

PEARL

Starter, free

The neutral baseline every run starts with - no perk, no penalty, just the feel of the ship.

AMBER

Reaches further for coins than any other ship, at the cost of a slightly bigger hitbox.

CRIMSON

Slips through gaps other ships can't fit through, trading away some shield capacity for it.

ELECTRIC

Bends time harder and longer whenever a slow-time coin lands.

TOXIC

Every gold coin buys more corridor room, though the bonus burns off faster too.

VOID

Every third shield coin gives two shields instead of one, at the cost of some margin on close calls.

NOVA

Every magnet coin pulls coins in for much longer, with less ammo held in reserve.

SOLARIS

Rare

The rarest ship in the roster - forgiving on close calls, built for players chasing the deepest runs.
