What the devlog is for

TUNL has a few unusual promises: a cave that is the same for every player on a given day, a ship that flies the same on every screen, and a difficulty that is measured rather than guessed. Each promise is easy to state and hard to keep, and most of the interesting work in the game is in keeping it. These posts are where I write down what broke, what I measured, and what I decided.

If you are here for the game rather than the engineering, the how-to-play guide covers the rules, the ships page covers the roster, and the changelog lists what changed in every version.
