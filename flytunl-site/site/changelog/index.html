TUNL Changelog

What changed in each version, newest first. The browser version at flytunl.ch/play always runs the newest build.

Changelog · updated 20 September 2026 · the latest version is 15.1

15.1: A small fix for coins

  • Boulders and mines now keep clear of every coin, so a power-up can no longer sit inside a rock or on top of a mine, out of reach. The cave is still the same for every player.

15.0: The run now begins outside the cave

  • Every run opens over the day's city at dusk and flies into a rock mouth. An "ENTERING THE TUNL" banner marks the way in. The cave, the score and the daily seed are exactly as before.
  • The ship in flight is drawn in a three-quarter side view: its wings sweep back with the speed, the hull rolls with your climb, and the warp ring gives a barrel roll. Hitbox unchanged.
  • The soft walls are gone. Walls are lethal from the tunnel mouth on, and the two hull scratches now last the whole run.
  • A sound and interface pass: distinct sounds for a hull scratch and a revive, stereo panning around the ship, an audible warning just before a cannon fires and before a loose stalactite falls, cave reverb on impacts, a low turbofan spool-up, three-level music and sound settings, and a pause when the app loses focus mid-run.
  • Local records were reset once, on first launch. Shards, stardust, ships, achievements and lifetime counters are untouched.

14.2: Music, the death screen and small feel fixes

  • Seamless music loops, with no audible jump.
  • The song's real ending plays when you die, then silence until the title music returns.
  • A longer death freeze frame, with the reticle on whatever hit you.
  • The bomb sound rebuilt as a thunder boom, and an engine spool-up that matches the launch.
  • Shooting a hazard now scores points, and the ammo coin is a brighter orange.

14.1: Sound and slow-time

  • A new in-game soundtrack.
  • Reworked impact sounds for the shield break, rock hits and mine and bomb explosions.
  • Picking up a blue coin now visibly slows the world: ripples, an ice-blue wash on the left, and slowed particles, exhaust and coin animations. Presentation only.

14.0: Paint and polish

  • Six hangar paint finishes, bought once with shards and equipped per ship. Purely cosmetic.
  • A full audio pass: a shared master bus with a soft limiter, and a rebalanced mix so that warnings are never quieter than a routine pickup.
  • A fix for the rendered wall pulling away from the screen edge when the corridor bonus was maxed or a warp was live.

13.0: A new look

  • New typography across every screen.
  • The title screen takes the colour of the day's world, and the TUNL wordmark was rebuilt.
  • A new score display: a gauge that fills toward your record, a combo chip beside the score, and a spark from each paying coin.
  • Every coin is drawn as the thing it does: an hourglass, a shield, a crosshair, a magnet, a bomb with a lit fuse, a poison flask, a whirlpool, a gem with outward arrows.
  • The death screen was rebuilt as a debriefing on a clear grid, with the score as its hero.

12.2: Leaderboard reset and an achievement fix

  • The all-time leaderboard was reset on both platforms, because its top scores were set on much easier builds. Daily and weekly boards were untouched.
  • "Master of the Fleet" now requires every ship in the roster to be maxed.

12.0 and 12.1: A gentler start and a plan for the run

  • A safe, hazard-free opening flight for the first stretch of every run.
  • The run is now a plan of seven-second sectors, each introducing one new thing, with a breather at the start of each. See the pacing devlog.
  • Two hull scratches to forgive early wall mistakes.
  • Boulders became rock islands with four outline families, and cannon shots got more flight time.
  • A faceted ship hull, and physics that no longer depends on the refresh rate.
  • A frozen death frame that points at what killed you.

11.0: The warp portal

  • A hoop hangs in the corridor now and then. Fly through it to surge ahead, untouchable, with gold coins pulled in automatically.
  • Ten new achievements.

10.5: Fairness across screens

  • Every screen size now flies the exact same daily cave. Four separate causes of per-device difference were found and fixed.
  • Boulders and falling stalactites moved much earlier, where players actually reach them.
  • A re-paced power-up economy, and two placement bugs fixed.

10.4: Flight-time achievements

  • Two new achievements for staying airborne for a minute and for two minutes in one run.
  • A fix for late-run power-up coins overshooting their intended share, after a player said there were too many shields.

10.3: Reachability

  • Ship unlock costs were cut hard, so the daily calendar rather than the shard count paces every ship.
  • The opening coins now teach releasing, not just holding.
  • World rank is hidden until enough players have flown the day's cave for it to mean something.

10.2: Thrust retune

  • The upward acceleration was eased after player feedback. See the story.

10.1: A bug fix

  • Achievements you had already earned before they existed are now granted retroactively.

10.0: Deep runs get variety

  • For runs past a few hundred points: a corridor whose shape changes character, pulses of extra speed, wide breather chambers, and a shifting colour palette.
  • A second hazard coin, the drain, which takes a share of your current score.
  • Lifetime distance on the title screen, and two lifetime-distance achievements.

9.1: The seven daily worlds

  • Eight new achievements built on the seven-day world rotation: one for a real run on each of the seven worlds, plus a Grand Tour for all of them.

9.0: Achievements

  • Eighteen achievements on Game Center and Play Games, unlocked by flying, upgrading ships, hitting milestones and returning daily.

8.4: Invite a friend

  • Invite a friend and earn a shard bonus when they fly their first cave, and a shared link now opens straight into the app if it is installed.

8.3: Daily reminder

  • An opt-in evening reminder on days you have not flown the day's tunnel, with one tap in Settings to turn it on or off.

8.1 and 8.2: Optional rewards

  • An optional video to continue a run, and a once-a-day optional video for a shard bonus.

Earlier versions

Versions before 8.1 built the core game: the hold-to-thrust flight, the daily cave, the first coin types, ships and shards, and the leaderboards.

For how the current game plays, see the how-to-play guide. If something in a new version broke your run, tell me at info@flytunl.ch.
