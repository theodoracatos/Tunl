TUNL - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

TUNL is a simple arcade game. This policy explains what data TUNL and its third-party services collect, and why.

Data collected directly by TUNL

TUNL stores your game progress, high scores, ship unlocks, and settings locally on your device. Two optional features - the open-web daily leaderboard and the "invite a friend" reward, both explained below - send a random, non-identifying device ID to TUNL's own backend. Nothing else about how you play ever leaves your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

TUNL shows interstitial ads between game sessions using Google AdMob. If you own the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase, ads are disabled entirely.

On iOS, TUNL asks for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before requesting personalized ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized using your device's advertising identifier.

If you are in the European Economic Area, UK, or Switzerland, TUNL shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads, letting you choose whether to allow personalized advertising.

AdMob may collect the following on our behalf:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / Advertising ID on Android), only with your consent
  • Approximate device and location information
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's User Consent Policy for details.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

On iOS and Android, TUNL uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how many people install the app after seeing a Google Ads campaign. This exists solely to tell us whether our advertising is working - it does not power any in-game feature, and TUNL has no account system for this data to attach to.

Firebase Analytics may collect on our behalf:

  • A device-scoped installation identifier, not linked to any personal identity
  • App-open, session, and in-app interaction events (e.g. when the app is first opened)
  • Approximate (city-level) location, derived from your IP address

In the EU, UK, and Switzerland, this collection requires your consent through the same form described above - if you decline, Firebase Analytics does not store or read identifiers on your device. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency choice additionally limits any collection linked to your advertising identifier. See Firebase's Privacy and Security page for details.

Website analytics (Cloudflare)

The TUNL website (flytunl.ch, including the playable page at flytunl.ch/play) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which countries and referring sites visitors come from. It is privacy-first by design: it sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, does not fingerprint your browser, and does not track you across other websites. The data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you, so no consent prompt is required. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.

In-app purchases

TUNL offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit / Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google and never reach TUNL's developer. TUNL only receives confirmation of whether you own the purchase, stored locally on your device.

Leaderboards (Game Center / Play Games)

If you sign in to Game Center (iOS) or Play Games (Android), your score and public gamertag/alias may be submitted to a leaderboard managed by Apple or Google, governed by their respective privacy policies. TUNL does not receive or store your Game Center / Play Games identity.

Daily leaderboard & referral rewards (TUNL's own backend)

The open-web version of TUNL (flytunl.ch/play) shows a live daily world rank, and on all three platforms - iOS, Android, and the open web - TUNL's optional "invite a friend" reward runs on TUNL's own backend, built on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1. This is infrastructure TUNL's developer operates directly, not a third-party advertising or analytics network - the only outside party involved is Cloudflare, acting purely as the hosting provider for that backend.

To support these two features, the following is sent there:

  • A random identifier generated on your device and stored locally (not linked to your name, email, or any account - TUNL has no account system)
  • Your score, for the day's leaderboard (open web only) or for the referral system's "did they actually play" check (all platforms)
  • If you send or follow an invite link, the same random identifiers for both the sender and the recipient, so a completed invite can be credited

Daily leaderboard scores are deleted after 45 days. An unclaimed referral reward is kept until it is claimed; claimed referral records are deleted after roughly 400 days. None of this data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond these two features.

Children's privacy

TUNL does not knowingly direct personalized advertising or data collection at children under the age required by applicable law in your region. Where required, ad requests are made non-personalized by default.

Your choices / requesting data deletion

TUNL has no account system, so nothing above can be tied to your real identity in the first place. Most of the data described above (your advertising identifier, approximate device/location, ad interaction data, and the Firebase installation, location, and session data) is collected and retained by Google directly (via AdMob and Firebase respectively), under Google's own retention schedule described in Google's Privacy Policy. To stop this collection or request its deletion:

  • iOS: go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and turn off tracking for TUNL - this limits both AdMob's and Firebase's identifier-linked collection. You can also reset your Advertising Identifier under Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Android: go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads on newer Android versions), then select "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID" - this limits both AdMob's and Firebase's identifier-linked collection.
  • Purchasing "Remove Ads" stops all future ad requests, and with them, all further data collection by AdMob.
  • Daily leaderboard / referral data: this is held on TUNL's own backend (see above), not Google's. Email info@flytunl.ch and we'll help you locate your device's random identifier and delete the matching records. Because this data is anonymous by design and not linked to any account, we can only act on requests specific enough to identify the record.

Changes to this policy
