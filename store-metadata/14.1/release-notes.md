# TUNL 14.1 - Play Console / App Store Connect "What's New" copy (15 locales)

Theme: sound + slow-time feel. Changes since 14.0 (commit e145755):

1. **New in-game soundtrack.** The play track was swapped for a new one (same composer),
   on iOS, Android and web (web m4a re-encoded); the music bed is louder.
2. **Reworked impact sounds.** Shield break, rock hits (bullets and cannon shots on wall
   or boulder), and mine and bomb explosions were rebuilt with real weight instead of
   noise-burst pops.
3. **Blue coin "Zeitblase".** Picking up a blue coin now visibly slows the world: ripples,
   an ice-blue wash on the left, slowed particles/exhaust/coin animations. Presentation
   only, no gameplay change.

Deliberately NOT in the copy: the rival death markers (memory says the worker is
undeployed - do not advertise it until it is live) and any dB or percentage numbers
(marketing rule: qualitative only). Every What's New locale is under Google Play's
500-character limit (checked by script, counts below), so the same copy doubles as App
Store Connect's "Neues in dieser Version". No em dashes (project rule).

Werbetexte (ASC promotional text) is unchanged - the core pitch has not moved since 9.1.
Re-pasted in full below anyway per the standing rule (it has reset to empty on ASC
across a version bump before).

## What's New / Release Notes

### en-US  (267 chars)
Fresh soundtrack, heavier blasts. TUNL has a new track for every run, and mines, bombs, shield breaks and rock hits now hit with real weight. Grab a blue coin and time itself bends: the cave slows around you in ripples and an icy glow, then surges back to full speed.

### de-DE  (341 chars)
Neuer Soundtrack, wuchtigere Explosionen. TUNL hat einen neuen Track für jeden Flug, und Minen, Bomben, Schildbrüche und Felstreffer schlagen jetzt mit echtem Gewicht ein. Schnapp dir eine blaue Münze und die Zeit selbst biegt sich: die Höhle wird um dich langsamer, mit Wellen und eisigem Schimmer, bevor sie wieder auf volle Fahrt anzieht.

### fr-FR  (346 chars)
Nouvelle bande-son, explosions plus lourdes. TUNL a un nouveau morceau pour chaque vol, et les mines, bombes, boucliers brisés et impacts de roche frappent désormais avec un vrai poids. Ramasse une pièce bleue et le temps lui-même se plie : la grotte ralentit autour de toi, avec des ondes et une lueur glacée, avant de repartir à pleine vitesse.

### it-IT  (319 chars)

Raccogli una moneta blu e il tempo stesso si piega: la grotta rallenta attorno a te, tra onde e un bagliore gelido, prima di tornare a piena velocità.

### es-ES  (348 chars)
