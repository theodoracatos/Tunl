# TUNL 18.1 - Play Console / App Store Connect copy (16 locales)

18.1 is the first update after 18.0 (live on both stores 2026-09-28). What changed for players:
- **Frenzy looks new** (218bcbd): a contour light stroked from the live 3D hull plus four-point
  glints at nose, wingtips and tail replace the ball of light; `FRENZY_SEC` 3.0 -> 3.5.
- **Wall pin fix** (d6e8d61): where the wall holds instead of kills (grace windows, warp, frenzy,
  the approach's mouth) the ship no longer "sticks" - it lets go the moment you steer.
- **HUD swap** (1df6792): hull plates move to the left next to the star meter, the magazine to
  the right; the hull row centres between the meter and the lane icons.
- Web-only commits since 18.0 (767c4f5, 4aec2bf, 0d61b48) are `isWeb()`-gated and not in the copy.
- The new App Store preview (the 18.0 montage) rides along: the ASC version prepared as 18.0.1
  is renamed to 18.1, previews and texts stay.

Deliberately NOT in the copy: numbers of any kind (feedback_marketing_no_stat_numbers). No em dashes.
Frenzy's name per locale = the one the 18.0 notes introduced.

Versions: iOS marketing 18.1 / build 63, Android versionName 18.1 / versionCode 56.

**ASC / Play status**: not yet submitted.

500 chars per language: every locale below is counted and stays under it.
