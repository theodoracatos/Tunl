# TUNL 14.2 - Play Console / App Store Connect "What's New" copy (15 locales)

Theme: music + death screen + small feel fixes. Changes since 14.1 (uncommitted working tree):

1. **Seamless music.** Play and title tracks loop on baked crossfade seams, no more audible jump.
2. **The song's real ending plays on death**, then silence until the title screen's music.
3. **Longer death freeze frame** (0.70s), reticle points at the cause.
4. **Bomb sound rebuilt** as a thunder boom; **engine spool-up** now lasts exactly as long as the launch ramp.
5. **Bullet hits score** (stalactite +1, cannon shot +2, mine +3); ammo coin is a brighter orange.

Deliberately NOT in the copy: exact point values and any dB numbers (qualitative only), rival
death markers (do not advertise until the worker is live). Every What's New locale is under
Google Play's 500-character limit (checked by script, counts below), so the same copy doubles
as App Store Connect's "Neues in dieser Version". No em dashes.

Werbetexte (ASC promotional text) is unchanged - the core pitch has not moved since 9.1.
Re-pasted in full below anyway per the standing rule.
