# TUNL 10.0 - App Store Connect copy (16 locales)

Theme: the deep-run variety system (score 900+: morphing corridor, speed pulses,
breather chambers, boulders, falling stalactites, coin-line shapes, palette drift),
the new drain coin (second hazard, steals run score), lifetime distance tracking on
the title screen, and two new lifetime-distance achievements (To the Moon / To the Sun).

Three fields, three purposes:
- **Werbetexte** (App Store Connect "Promotional Text" only, 170-char limit) - the ASC
  10.0 version reset this field to EMPTY for every locale (it does NOT carry forward
  from 9.1 automatically, contrary to what was assumed after 9.1 - correcting that for
  next release). Content is the same evergreen tagline copy from 9.1, just re-pasted.
- **Beschreibung** (App Store Connect "Description") - kept the existing evocative
  opening two paragraphs (proven copy, unchanged), and appended three new paragraphs
  covering coins/hazards, the deep-run system, and ships/achievements/leaderboard - the
  full feature set was previously undersold on the product page.
- **Neues in dieser Version** (What's New, version-specific) - real 10.0 content, under
  500 chars/locale so it doubles as Google Play release notes if needed.

No em dashes anywhere (project rule) - hyphen-minus with spaces instead.
pt-BR reuses pt per the established convention (8.3/8.4/9.0/9.1).
zh = Chinese Traditional (the only Chinese locale active in ASC for this app).
