# TUNL 9.0 - Release notes (16 locales)

Theme: Achievements (Game Center / Play Games) - 18 of them, unlocked by flying,
upgrading ships, hitting milestone scores, and returning daily for a streak. Also
ships a per-ship engine sound rework (each ship's thruster now has its own distinct
sound instead of sharing one texture) - folded into the same copy rather than called
out as a separate line, matching the "two hooks max" pattern from 8.4. Not mentioned
in the public copy: the `tunl_highscore_alltime_v2` leaderboard cutover (invisible to
players - old board's scores just stop counting) and the Beat My Score challenge image
refresh (promotional asset, not a changelog item). All entries comfortably under
Google Play's 500-char/locale limit. en/de/fr/it/es/pt/ja reuse the flytunl.ch
"New in 9.0" homepage banner copy (`flytunl-site/i18n/home.json` hero.newBody); pt-BR
reuses pt per the 8.3/8.4 convention.
