**What's New**

```
A tidier debriefing. On smaller phones, a run that earns a pile of rewards no longer stacks them over the buttons: what doesn't fit moves to the free space on the right.

And your first flight of the day no longer calls itself a daily best. There was nothing to beat yet.
```

**Werbetexte**

```
One cave a day, the same one for every player on Earth. Take off over the city at dusk, dive into the rock, and see how deep you get before it is gone forever.
