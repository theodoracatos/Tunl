# For 19.9 (queued 2026-10-13): App Store names, subtitles and keywords in all 18 listing locales

Values: `aso-names-2026-10-13.json` (this folder), lengths checked (name/subtitle <= 30, keywords <= 100,
no keyword repeats a name/subtitle word of the same locale). 19.8 was WAITING_FOR_REVIEW, which locks
name/subtitle (appInfo) and keywords (version), so this waits for the next version.

**Google Play titles were changed the same day** (15 Play languages, same names as the ASC `name` column;
ru kept as `TUNL: Самолёт в пещере`). Play has no subtitle/keyword fields.

## Why

TUNL ranks for nothing but its own name. The names spent their characters on words nobody searches.
Measured 2026-10-13 (App Store search hints = what people type, iTunes Search API = who ranks, with
`userRatingCount` per storefront because ratings are per country):

- **No search demand for the game's own words in any of the 16 languages:** "cave/Höhle/grotte/cueva/洞窟",
  "tunnel" (returns VPN apps everywhere), "one tap", "endless". TUNL ranks 4-22 for "höhlen spiel"/"пещера"/
  "洞穴"/"동굴", which nobody types.
- **The demand that fits TUNL exists in every language in three forms:** plane games ("flugzeug spiele",
  "jeux d'avion", "juegos de aviones", "飛行機ゲーム", "비행기 게임", "uçak oyunları", "game pesawat",
  "samoloty gra", "العاب طيارات"), fighter-jet games (TUNL flies an F-14: "kampfjet spiele", "avion de chasse",
  "jogo de avião de caça", "戦闘機ゲーム", "savaş uçağı oyunu", "pesawat tempur", "طيارات حربية") and
  offline / no-internet games, plus their combinations.
- **A name alone does not win a head term:** the ru name `Самолёт в пещере` went live 2026-10-13 and is indexed
  (rank 1 for the phrase), yet TUNL is outside the top 100 for "самолёт" (top 10 hold 2-4k ratings). Names win
  where few apps match all words of a query. Such gaps (n = apps matching, med = median ratings of the top 10):
  - DE "kampfjet spiele offline" n=4; "flugzeug spiele ohne internet" n=11; AT/CH plane terms med 170-670
    (DE 4500) because ratings are per country.
  - BR "jogo de avião de caça" n=32, med 0.5. PL "samolot gra" med 20, "samoloty gra" n=15.
  - ES "juegos de aviones sin internet" n=18, med 105. ID "game pesawat tempur offline" n=15, med 79.
  - IN "fighter jet games offline" n=25, "helicopter games offline" n=23. SA "العاب طيارات بدون نت" n=14.
  - Helicopter games are a weak niche nearly everywhere (KR med 15, GR 22, PL 25, ID 48, AT 46, TW 99) -
    kept as a keyword (the classic "helicopter game" genre TUNL belongs to), never in a visible name.
  - US/GB English plane/jet/offline terms are saturated (US med 24k-75k): en-US/en-GB matter for the many
    storefronts that index en-GB first (India, Indonesia, Türkiye, Poland, Greece, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt,
    Kazakhstan ...), not for the US.
- **Storefront indexing (Apple's table):** all of Latin America indexes es-MX (not es-ES); Switzerland indexes
  de + en-GB + fr + it; Japan ja + en-US; Russia ru + en-GB. The en-GB keywords keep `самолет,игра` for the
  CIS storefronts that index only en-GB.

## Offline claim

The subtitles say offline / without internet in most locales. Verified 2026-10-13 on the Android emulator
(Tunl_Pixel, debug build of the current tree): airplane mode on (`ping` unreachable), fresh install,
cold launch -> reminder prompt -> title "World 651" (day from the device clock) -> city -> cave -> death at 28
-> debriefing. iOS loads the same bundle (`GameView.swift` `loadFileURL`); leaderboards, ads and sharing are the
only network parts (code check 2026-10-11). An airplane-mode run on an iPhone is still worth doing once.
