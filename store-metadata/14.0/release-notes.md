# TUNL 14.0 - Play Console / App Store Connect "What's New" copy (15 locales)

Theme: paint + polish. Changes since 13.0 (commit f272da2):

1. **Hangar liveries.** Six cosmetic paint finishes (FACTORY free, STEALTH, STRIPE,
   SPLIT, CHROME, AURORA), bought once with shards in a new Paint shop opened from the
   ALL SHIPS sheet (unlocks once AMBER is owned). Equipped per ship, so every ship in
   the hangar keeps its own look. Purely cosmetic - no perk, hitbox, placement or
   leaderboard effect.
2. **Full audio pass.** Master bus + soft-clip limiter, the whole mix raised and
   rebalanced so shield breaks, cannon fire, near-misses and combos are no longer
   quieter than a routine gold-coin pickup; a new explosion sound on mine contact; music
   now loops cleanly instead of replaying its own fade-out.
3. **Off-screen wall fix.** A maxed corridor bonus or a warp could previously push the
   rendered wall past the edge of the screen, leaving a bare strip; the rock now always
   reaches the screen edge. Visual only, not in store copy (too small to be worth a
   sentence to players).

Every What's New locale is under Google Play's 500-character limit (checked by script,
counts below), so the same copy doubles as App Store Connect's "Neues in dieser Version".
No em dashes (project rule). No stat numbers (marketing rule) - the audio pass is
described qualitatively ("louder", "balanced"), never with the measured dB deltas from
CLAUDE.md.

Werbetexte (ASC promotional text) is unchanged - the core pitch has not moved since 9.1.
Re-pasted in full below anyway per the standing rule (it has reset to empty on ASC
across a version bump before).
