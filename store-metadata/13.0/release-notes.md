# TUNL 13.0 - Play Console / App Store Connect "What's New" copy (15 locales)

Theme: a new look. Everything in this release is presentation - no difficulty, physics,
scoring or economy change. Changes since 12.2 (commits c6521b3, 022001e, plus the coin redesign):

1. **New typography.** Courier New replaced by Chakra Petch (text) and JetBrains Mono
   (numbers), bundled with the game, on every screen.
2. **Title screen in the day's colour.** Logo, world line, buttons and a floor light under
   the ship take the colour of today's planet; the TUNL wordmark was rebuilt.
3. **Score HUD.** A gauge under the score fills toward the all-time record (orange once on
   fire, gold past the record); the combo multiplier moved into a chip beside the score
   with a countdown bar; paying coins send a spark into the score.
4. **Coins show what they do.** Every coin is drawn as its effect instead of a glossy gem
   with a pictogram: magnet, hourglass (slow time), shield, crosshair (ammo), bomb with a
   lit fuse, poison flask, drain whirlpool, gold gem with outward arrows. Shield is now
   violet and bomb red (swapped). The in-game info panel, the mission label for shield
   coins and flytunl.ch (homepage + how-to-play) describe them the same way.
5. **Small-screen fixes.** World banner and milestone flash never cover the score block
   (was overlapping on iPhone 12 mini / SE).

Web-only (flytunl.ch/play, not in store copy): a frame in the day's colour around the
playfield on desktop.

Every What's New locale is under Google Play's 500-character limit (checked by script,
counts below), so the same copy doubles as App Store Connect's "Neues in dieser Version".
No em dashes (project rule). No stat numbers (marketing rule).

Werbetexte (ASC promotional text) is unchanged - the core pitch has not moved. Re-pasted
in full below anyway per the standing rule (it has reset to empty on ASC across a version
bump before).
